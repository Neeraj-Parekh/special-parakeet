01 · PROBLEM STATEMENT

Cash-on-delivery is India's largest unpriced credit risk.

Every COD order extends a stranger a 3–7 day uncollateralized loan. When it comes back, the merchant pays — courier both ways, refund, refund, inventory stuck for weeks.
