01 · PROBLEM STATEMENT — WHY NOW

Three clocks converged in 18 months.

OCT 2025

NPCI OC-201B — UPI Circle

Delegated payments got hard caps: ₹5,000 per transaction, ₹15,000 per month, 5 devices, 24h cooling. Any AI agent moving money on UPI must live inside these rails — most agent demos ignore them.

JUN 2026

RBI draft Model Risk Management guidance

Tamper-evident audit trails, human-in-the-loop overrides, red-teaming, kill switches — mandated for every AI model in Indian finance. Today's black-box RTO shields cannot comply.

2025–26

Zero-MDR economics

>96% of consumer UPI payments are under ₹2,000 — payment margin is ~zero (PCI estimates ₹5,500 Cr/yr subsidy). Platform value moved to merchant services: Paytm's own pivot — 13.7M merchant device subscriptions, merchant lending, agentic AI.

The window: whoever builds trust infrastructure for merchant-side AI now sets the standard the regulation will demand.
