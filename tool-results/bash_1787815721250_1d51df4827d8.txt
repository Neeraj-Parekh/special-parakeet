Discovered + fixed during test-driven iteration: my initial code stored `p_min = p+σ` (treating p_min as the minimum of the (p+σ) series), but the survey §3.2 stores `p_min = p` (the error rate at the minimum point, with σ_min separately tracking the std at that point — so `p_min + σ_min` IS the (p+σ) at the minimum).
